**Physics BTech PYQ - Previous Year Question Paper**
=====================================================

**Section A: Short Answers (5 marks each)**

### Question 1
What is the principle behind a solar cell, and how does it convert sunlight into electrical energy?

---

### Question 2
Describe the concept of wave-particle duality in physics. Provide an example to illustrate this phenomenon.

---

### Question 3
Explain the difference between capacitive reactance (Xc) and inductive reactance (XL). Use a simple circuit diagram to support your answer.

---

### Question 4
What is the significance of the Planck constant (h) in quantum mechanics? How does it relate to energy levels?

---

### Question 5
Describe the properties of an ideal gas, including its behavior under different conditions. Provide equations to support your answer.

---

**Section B: Medium Answers (10 marks each)**

### Question 1
A 50 W light bulb is connected in series with a 100 Ω resistor. Calculate the current drawn by the bulb when it is operating at 220 V. Assume that the bulb's resistance remains constant over time.

---

### Question 2
Two electrons are moving in opposite directions along a wire, each having a velocity of 10^4 m/s. If they collide head-on, calculate the kinetic energy transferred to the collision using the relativistic formula for kinetic energy.

---

### Question 3
A 5 kg block is suspended from a spring with a spring constant (k) of 100 N/m. The block undergoes simple harmonic motion when released from rest at x = 0. Calculate the maximum displacement and velocity of the block, assuming that it oscillates about the equilibrium position.

---

**Section C: Long/Essay Answers (20 marks each)**

### Question 1
Discuss the concept of blackbody radiation in thermodynamics. Explain how Planck's equation relates to this phenomenon, and provide examples of how it has been used in real-world applications.

---

### Question 2
Describe the principles behind a particle accelerator, including the role of electromagnetic waves and high-energy collisions. Discuss the significance of particle accelerators in modern physics research, such as particle physics and materials science.
